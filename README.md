# Honeycomb-Clicker
A 3D CAD model of a magnetic fidget clicker with hexagonal designs all around the model. 

The inspiration for this model came from my wish to use magnets on my first ever CAD fidget. I searched online for examples, and came across other clickers, who used protruding parts to create a click. However, I was curious on how a magnet will feel, so I decided to recreate the classic clicker fidget with magnets instead. On the way, I also decided to add some flare with hexagonal cutouts. Here, I currently have the Honeycomb v3.6 clickers, with 2x4 and 2x5 mm magnet sizes. I also plan on adding many other versions! 

!IMPORTANT!
1. When assembling, please match the side of the switch that was printed directly on top of the building plate with the notch/cut-out on the middle triangle of the case. Otherwise, the swinging mechanism will be very rough due to friction.
2. Also, after putting in the 2 magnets for the switch, simply place the remaining 2 magnets so that it sticks with the switch magnets. Next, snap the switch in to the case in the correct orientation mentioned in number 1. Finally, simply move the switch side to side to insert the magnets into the case rather than struggling to put the magnets into the case directly.
3.
